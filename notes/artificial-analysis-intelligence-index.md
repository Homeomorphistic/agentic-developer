# Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index

Research checked on 2026-08-23 against Artificial Analysis's first-party pages.

## Findings

- The Intelligence Index is a composite benchmark for comparing language-model
  capabilities across reasoning, knowledge, mathematics, programming, and
  agentic work. Artificial Analysis runs the evaluations independently and
  combines them into a 0–100 score. [Index page][index]
- Version 4.1.1 combines nine evaluations: GDPval-AA v2, τ³-Banking,
  Terminal-Bench v2.1, SciCode, AA-LCR, AA-Omniscience, Humanity's Last Exam,
  GPQA Diamond, and CritPt. [Methodology][methodology]
- The category weights are Agents 34%, Coding 24%, Scientific Reasoning 24%,
  and General 18%. [Methodology][methodology]
- Higher scores indicate stronger aggregate performance, but the index is
  primarily text-based and English-language, and a specific benchmark may be
  more relevant to a particular use case. [Index page][index]
- The slide image is a reduced screenshot of the live score chart on the
  official index page. Because the leaderboard changes as models are added or
  re-evaluated, the linked live chart is the authoritative current view.

[index]: https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/artificial-analysis-intelligence-index
[methodology]: https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/intelligence-benchmarking
